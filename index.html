02 Team news, players, tactics & motivation

Consider injuries/suspensions, goalkeeper and centre-backs, player quality, depth, rest, rotation, tactical matchup and what each team is playing for.

03 Pass A — V3.0 statistical/model analysis

Pass A uses opponent-adjusted last-five attacking xG and defensive xGA. Expected goals are a transparent starting formula, not a proven calibration: λH = 0.55 × H attack + 0.45 × A xGA + home advantage + H context; λA = 0.55 × A attack + 0.45 × H xGA + A context. Home advantage starts at +0.20 goals and is not claimed to be backtested. Neutral venues remove it.

04 Pass B — independent second assessment

Do independent research / estimate first. Pass A's forecast numbers stay hidden in this section until you reveal Pass C. Enter your own λ estimates and sources before comparing.

Finish and save Pass A first to open Pass B. Do not copy the Pass A numbers into this independent pass.

05 Pass C — compare, debate & final call

The default final λ is the simple midpoint between Pass A and B. This is a transparent starting rule, not a backtested optimal blend. Review disagreement; override only with a written evidence-based reason.

Complete Pass A and the independent Pass B before starting the debate.

Backup and restore

Fixtures and analysis are stored on this device/browser. Export a JSON backup regularly. Clearing browser storage or uninstalling the app may remove local data.

This version doesn't connect to sports APIs and does not confirm source URLs are true. The model's opponent-strength adjustment and home-advantage starting value have not been validated by a historical backtest within this app. The AI Council requires at least three independently parsed forecasts plus a saved Round 2 cross-examination before Pass C/qualification. Don't treat a displayed probability as guaranteed or as a betting instruction.

Practical research workflow

  1. Verify the official fixture list, date, kick-off, league/cup and senior/youth status.
  2. Check current league table, matches played, points per game and schedule context.
  3. Research last five results, opponent strength, xG/xGA (and npxG when available); enter source URLs and date.
  4. Check injuries/suspensions, goalkeeper and centre-backs, key-player ratings, bench quality, rotation, rest, manager/tactics and motivation.
  5. Complete Pass A then hide its forecasts and make Pass B independently from other sources.
  6. In Pass C, compare them sequentially, resolve disagreement with evidence, record uncertainty, then review O/U 2.5, BTTS and scoreline probabilities.
  7. Use the strongest-pick funnel. If evidence is incomplete or nothing qualifies, record that honestly.
